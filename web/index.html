What does this check?

The mainnet check shows: EIP-7702 delegation and its target contract, nonce, native balance, main ERC-20 balances and ERC-20 approvals granted by direct transactions in the last 90 days that are still open. It cannot see approvals granted by off-chain signatures (permits). Results are cached for 10 minutes.

FAQ

What is a sweeper?

A sweeper is an automated bot run by whoever stole your private key. It watches the wallet and moves out any ETH the moment it arrives, so you can never pay the gas to move your remaining tokens yourself. It may also install an EIP-7702 delegation on your address.

What can be rescued and what cannot?

The verified tokens (16 on Base, 12 on Arbitrum, 8 on OP Mainnet) are the core of every rescue. Other ERC-20 tokens in your wallet are detected automatically during the scan and can be selected by you to attempt rescue at the same fee percentage, without guarantee. NFTs are not rescued yet. Funds that were already stolen cannot be recovered.

How does it work?

Scan the public address, enter a safe wallet and review a simulation with the exact amounts and fee, then sign locally. Our relayer pays the gas and submits one transaction that moves the tokens to your safe wallet. No ETH is ever sent to the compromised wallet, so the sweeper has nothing to grab.

Does my private key leave my browser?

No. Only the key of the already-compromised wallet is used (the attacker already has it), never your seed phrase or your safe wallet’s key. It is used only in memory inside this browser tab to sign and is never sent, stored or logged; only signatures reach our relayer. Proof: the exact signing code on GitHub. Check it yourself in DevTools → Network, and see the verified contract on Basescan (address listed below). UNSWEEP never writes to you first.

How much does it cost?

A share of the value rescued: 15% below 1,000 USD, 10% from 1,000 to 10,000 USD and 5% above 10,000 USD. The fee comes out of the rescued tokens in the same transaction and is shown in the simulation before you sign. Nothing is paid upfront: if the rescue does not execute, you pay nothing.

Which tokens and networks are supported?

Rescue works on Base, Arbitrum and OP Mainnet for the ERC-20 tokens listed under Supported tokens on this page (16 on Base, 12 on Arbitrum, 8 on OP Mainnet). Ethereum mainnet is a free read-only check of delegation, nonce, balances and open approvals.

Can you rescue airdrops or vesting tokens?

Sometimes. Every airdrop or vesting contract is different, so they are reviewed case by case. Email support@unsweep.com with your public address and the name of the claim or vesting contract. Never send keys or seed phrases.

What happens with rescues above 10,000 USD?

They run automatically up to 100,000 USD per rescue, with extra safety checks that take a few seconds: fresh Chainlink prices for every token, stablecoin prices within a safe band and a shorter list of eligible tokens. Other tokens are limited to 10,000 USD per rescue.

How do I tell real support from scammers?

The only site is unsweep.com and the only contact is support@unsweep.com, which only replies to emails you send. We never DM you first on Reddit, Telegram, Discord or X, never ask for your seed phrase or for any key by message and never ask for payment upfront (the only key ever used is the compromised one, typed by you in Step 3 on unsweep.com). Anyone who does is a scammer.

My wallet was just hacked. What should I do right now?

Stop sending funds to the compromised wallet. Create a new wallet with a new seed phrase, ideally on a clean device. Scan your address above to see what is left, then follow the compromised wallet checklist in the guides below.

Why does speed matter?

While you wait for manual help, the attacker can come back and you can make costly mistakes. UNSWEEP is fully automatic: no signup, no forms, no waiting for a human.

What does Step 1 (Free Wallet Diagnostic) check?

Step 1 is 100% free and read-only: you only enter a public address, with no signing and no private key. On Base, Arbitrum and OP Mainnet it checks ETH balance, nonce, active EIP-7702 delegation and ERC-20 tokens (verified and additional detected tokens). On Ethereum mainnet it runs a read-only check of delegation, nonce, balances and open ERC-20 approvals from the last 90 days.

How does the diagnostic identify EIP-7702 delegations and sweepers?

When an EIP-7702 delegation is active, Step 1 checks it against known legitimate delegates (MetaMask Smart Account, Coinbase EIP7702Proxy, Ambire, Alchemy and UNSWEEP) versus unknown contracts, and links the delegate address directly to Basescan, Arbiscan, Etherscan or Optimistic Etherscan (OP Mainnet) so you can inspect its code. Showing no delegation does not rule out a classic sweeper bot or a leaked private key.
