UNSWEEP

Mainnet: read-only check. Rescue is testnet beta only.

We never ask for your private key to scan, and never by chat, email or DM. We will never DM you first. We never ask for your seed phrase.

Step 1 · Scan (public address only)

Enter a public address. No key and no signature are needed to scan.

The mainnet check shows: EIP-7702 delegation and its target contract, nonce, native balance, main ERC-20 balances and ERC-20 approvals granted by direct transactions in the last 90 days that are still open. It cannot see approvals granted by off-chain signatures (permits). Results are cached for 10 minutes.

Step 2 · Simulation: what you receive and the fee (testnet only)

Enter the safe wallet that will receive the assets.

Step 3 · Sign locally and rescue (testnet beta only)

  • Your key is used only inside this browser tab, in memory, to sign. It is never sent, stored or logged. Only signatures go to our relayer.
  • Seed phrases (12/24 words) are rejected. Paste only the 64-hex private key of the compromised testnet wallet.
  • Prefer offline signing? Open this page offline in a separate tab (option D, coming soon).
