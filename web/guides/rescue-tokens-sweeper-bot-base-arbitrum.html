How to rescue tokens from a wallet with a sweeper bot on Base and Arbitrum, step by step

This guide is informational. Funds already taken from a compromised wallet cannot be recovered, NFTs are not rescued, and no rescue is guaranteed to succeed.

Current scope of UNSWEEP: Base: 16 verified tokens. Arbitrum: 12 verified tokens. Other ERC-20 tokens may be detected, but without any guarantee. Ethereum: read-only diagnostic only (no rescue). Funds already taken cannot be recovered, and NFTs are not rescued. See what can and cannot be rescued and the step-by-step rescue guide. Start with the free diagnostic.

Before you start

  • You need control of the compromised wallet’s private key, because step 3 is signed locally with it.
  • You need a separate, safe destination wallet that the attacker has never had access to.
  • Do not send gas (ETH) to the compromised wallet first: a sweeper bot usually takes it. See why sending gas is risky.

Step 1 · Check your wallet

Open the free diagnostic at https://unsweep.com/ and enter the compromised address. This step is read-only: no keys and no signatures. It shows what is detected on Base and Arbitrum (and, on Ethereum, a read-only view).

Step 2 · Simulation: what you receive and the fee

Review the simulation: which tokens would be moved, what the destination wallet would receive and the fee. If anything looks wrong, stop here; nothing has been signed.

Step 3 · Sign locally and rescue

Sign locally and submit. The rescue is designed to move the tokens in a single transaction, so the compromised wallet never sits holding gas that a sweeper bot could take first. Success is not guaranteed.

Eligibility limit

A limit of 100,000 USD applies to eligibility for a rescue. It is an eligibility limit, not a guarantee: it does not promise that any amount will be rescued or that a rescue will succeed.

After the rescue

Treat the compromised wallet as permanently unsafe and stop using it. If the wallet has a malicious EIP-7702 delegation, read how to detect and mitigate it.

Before you start: trust facts

Sounds familiar? “My funds disappear as soon as they arrive” · “I can’t pay for gas because the ETH is taken instantly” · “My wallet was hacked and I can’t get my tokens out”.

  • Open source: github.com/unsweep/unsweep. Rescue contract 0x38bcF425bC4d13624734e09376F2fafc799fA8c8, source verified on Sourcify (exact match): Base · Arbitrum · Basescan.
  • Which key, and where it goes: a rescue uses only the private key of the wallet that is already compromised, the one the attacker already has. Never your seed phrase and never the key of your new safe wallet. The key signs 100% inside your browser and is never sent to UNSWEEP or any server; only signatures are sent (see the exact signing code on GitHub). Check it yourself: open DevTools → Network before signing, and see the verified contract on Basescan. UNSWEEP never writes to you first.
  • We never DM you: UNSWEEP never contacts anyone by DM, Telegram or Discord. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Cost: free diagnostic, no upfront payment. Success fee only if the rescue executes, taken from the rescued tokens in the same transaction: 15% (<1,000 USD), 10% (1,000–10,000 USD), 5% (>10,000 USD); hard 15% maximum in the contract; shown in a simulation before you sign.
  • Cannot rescue: funds already taken, NFTs, anything on Ethereum mainnet (read-only). Tokens outside the verified lists may be detected without guarantee. Nothing is guaranteed.
