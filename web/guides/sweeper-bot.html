Before you start: trust facts

Sounds familiar? “My funds disappear as soon as they arrive” · “I can’t pay for gas because the ETH is taken instantly” · “My wallet was hacked and I can’t get my tokens out”.

  • Open source: github.com/unsweep/unsweep. Rescue contract 0x38bcF425bC4d13624734e09376F2fafc799fA8c8, source verified on Sourcify (exact match): Base · Arbitrum · Basescan.
  • Which key, and where it goes: a rescue uses only the private key of the wallet that is already compromised, the one the attacker already has. Never your seed phrase and never the key of your new safe wallet. The key signs 100% inside your browser and is never sent to UNSWEEP or any server; only signatures are sent (see the exact signing code on GitHub). Check it yourself: open DevTools → Network before signing, and see the verified contract on Basescan. UNSWEEP never writes to you first.
  • We never DM you: UNSWEEP never contacts anyone by DM, Telegram or Discord. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Cost: free diagnostic, no upfront payment. Success fee only if the rescue executes, taken from the rescued tokens in the same transaction: 15% (<1,000 USD), 10% (1,000–10,000 USD), 5% (>10,000 USD); hard 15% maximum in the contract; shown in a simulation before you sign.
  • Cannot rescue: funds already taken, NFTs, anything on Ethereum mainnet (read-only). Tokens outside the verified lists may be detected without guarantee. Nothing is guaranteed.
