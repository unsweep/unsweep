How to check if a wallet has a sweeper bot

UNSWEEP will never DM you first or ask for your seed phrase or private key. This page is informational; nothing on it is a guarantee of success.

You can check most signs with a block explorer and the public address only. Do not send ETH "to test": if a bot is present, it will be lost.

Signs to look for

  • Past incoming ETH left the wallet within seconds or a few blocks, in a transaction sent from your address that you did not sign.
  • Those outgoing transfers go repeatedly to the same unknown address.
  • Tokens are still present but the ETH balance is always near zero.
  • Transactions you did not sign appear in the history (any outgoing transaction from your address was signed with its key).

Also check for a delegation

Read the account code: if it starts with 0xef0100, the wallet has an EIP-7702 delegation (sweeper bot vs delegation).

Run the free diagnostic

The free diagnostic is read-only: enter the public address, with no keys and no signatures. It shows the tokens found, the delegation state and whether a rescue is in scope. It is informational and not a guarantee.

Scope: Base: 16 verified tokens. Arbitrum: 12 verified tokens. Other ERC-20 tokens may be detected, without guarantee. Ethereum: read-only diagnostic only (no rescue). Funds already taken cannot be recovered and NFTs are not rescued.

FAQ

How can I tell if a sweeper bot is on my wallet?

Look in a block explorer for incoming ETH that left within seconds in transactions from your address that you did not sign, often to the same address.

Should I send a small amount of ETH to test?

No. If a bot is present, the ETH will be lost.

Does the diagnostic need my private key?

No. The diagnostic is read-only and only needs the public address. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

Does a clean history mean the wallet is safe?

Not necessarily. If the key or seed phrase may have been exposed, treat the wallet as compromised.
