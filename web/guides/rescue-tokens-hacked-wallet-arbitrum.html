Rescue tokens from a hacked wallet on Arbitrum

UNSWEEP will never DM you first or ask for your seed phrase or private key. This page is informational; nothing on it is a guarantee of success.

On Arbitrum One (chain id 42161) the problem is the same: the attacker holds the key, so any ETH sent for gas is swept and the tokens stay stuck.

Verified tokens on Arbitrum

12 verified tokens: USDC, USDT, DAI, WETH, cbETH, LINK, GHO, tBTC, sUSDe, COMP, wstETH, weETH. Other ERC-20 tokens may be detected by the scan and can be selected to attempt a rescue at the same fee percentage, without guarantee.

Steps

  1. Stop sending ETH to the compromised address.
  2. Create a new wallet on a trusted device to use as the safe destination.
  3. Select Arbitrum in the free diagnostic and enter the public address.
  4. Review the simulation: amounts you will receive and the fee.
  5. Sign locally. A relayer submits the EIP-7702 transaction and pays the gas.

Verify the contracts

Both contracts are deployed with CREATE2 at the same address on Base and Arbitrum: delegate 0x38bcF425bC4d13624734e09376F2fafc799fA8c8, config 0x56a345661e3999F2b20f1E069aAC14097Fa0D118. You can check them on Arbiscan and in the repository.

Scope: Base: 16 verified tokens. Arbitrum: 12 verified tokens. Other ERC-20 tokens may be detected, without guarantee. Ethereum: read-only diagnostic only (no rescue). Funds already taken cannot be recovered and NFTs are not rescued.

FAQ

Which tokens can be rescued on Arbitrum?

12 verified tokens: USDC, USDT, DAI, WETH, cbETH, LINK, GHO, tBTC, sUSDe, COMP, wstETH, weETH. Other ERC-20 tokens may be detected, without guarantee.

Are the contracts the same as on Base?

Yes. They are deployed with CREATE2 at the same addresses on Base and Arbitrum.

Can funds already stolen be recovered?

No. A rescue only applies to tokens still in the wallet.

Is success guaranteed?

No. It depends on the state of the wallet and the network; check the simulation before signing.
