# UNSWEEP Partner Kit: Commercial and Referral Reference

Status: public reference material for content creators, security bloggers, community moderators, educators and tool authors who want to recommend UNSWEEP in their own content. It contains no outreach templates. UNSWEEP does not cold-message anyone, and linking to it is voluntary.

Website: https://unsweep.com/

## 1. Program status (please read first)

- Linking to UNSWEEP is free and needs no approval, application, call or agreement.
- A paid referral commission is not available today. There is currently no referral tracking, attribution or payout of any kind. Please do not tell your audience that you earn a commission from UNSWEEP.
- If a referral program is launched in the future, its terms will be published on unsweep.com. Nothing in this kit promises that it will exist, or what it would pay.

## 2. What your readers get

UNSWEEP is a self-service, non-custodial tool that helps people move the ERC-20 tokens still left in a compromised wallet, for example one drained by a sweeper bot or a malicious EIP-7702 delegation, to a safe address they control.

- Free diagnostic: enter the compromised address on https://unsweep.com/ and see what remains and whether it is in scope.
- Base: operational for rescue, 16 verified ERC-20 tokens.
- Arbitrum: operational for rescue, 12 verified ERC-20 tokens.
- Ethereum: read-only (balances can be checked, but no rescues).
- Other ERC-20 tokens may be included, but they are unverified and carry no guarantee.
- The key is signed locally in the browser and is not sent to UNSWEEP.

## 3. What it costs your readers

The diagnostic is free. A success fee is charged only on a completed rescue and is taken from the rescued value:

| Rescued value | Fee |
|---|---|
| Under 1,000 USD | 15% |
| 1,000 to 10,000 USD | 10% |
| Above 10,000 USD | 5% |

There are no upfront payments or subscriptions. Mentioning UNSWEEP or using a link never changes the fee a user pays.

## 4. What it cannot do

Be explicit about these limits so that nobody is misled:

- NFTs are not rescued.
- Funds that have already been stolen cannot be recovered.
- Ethereum mainnet is read-only, so there are no rescues there.
- 100,000 USD is a per-rescue eligibility limit, not a guarantee of recovery. Rescues above it are refused, and rescues above 10,000 USD go through extra checks and may be refused.

## 5. Rules for anyone recommending UNSWEEP

- No spam and no unsolicited direct messages to victims. Victims are often contacted by scammers, so an unexpected DM, even a well-meant one, looks like another scam.
- Never ask anyone for a seed phrase or private key, and never suggest that UNSWEEP staff will ask for one.
- No impersonation, and no paid ads that use the UNSWEEP name or bid on other brands.
- Do not promise recovery, amounts or timings.
- Do not claim audits, alliances, endorsements, clients or statistics on UNSWEEP's behalf. None are claimed in this kit.

## 7. Reader FAQ you can quote

- Is it free to check? Yes, the diagnostic is free.
- Do I give my key to UNSWEEP? No. Signing happens locally in your browser.
- Can it get back what was already stolen? No. It only moves what is still in the wallet.
- Can it rescue NFTs? No.
- Is support available? Yes, in writing and asynchronously only.
